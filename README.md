# US_Helathcare_Case_Study
## Objective:
This project analyzes a US healthcare dataset containing patient demographics, admission details, medical conditions, billing information, and insurance data from 100 hospitals. The objective is to derive actionable insights for healthcare executives through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization, and business recommendations.
## Dataset Used:
<a href="https://github.com/Deepak-NEGII/Data_Analysis_Dashboard/blob/main/Healthcare_Data_v1%20(1).csv">Dataset</a>
## Questions
- Analyze medical condition prevalence across demographic groups.
- Identify key factors affecting patient healthcare costs and suggest cost optimization strategies.
- Analyze admission trends and provide recommendations for hospital resource planning and workload management.

## Process
- Verify data for any missing values and anomalies, and sort out the same.
- Made sure data is consistent and clean with respect to data type, data format and values used.
- Created pivot tables according to the questions asked.
- Merge all pivot tables into one dashboard and apply slicer to make dynamic.
## Project Insights
- Arthritis and Cancer prevalence increase with age.
- Asthma is most common among young adults (18–37 years).
- Senior females (60–80 years) show the highest risk of Diabetes.
- Hypertension is more prevalent among middle-aged males, while Obesity is more common among middle-aged females.
- Insurance providers significantly impact patient expenses, indicating opportunities for cost optimization through better insurance selection.
- Patients can reduce costs by choosing suitable insurance plans and prioritizing preventive healthcare.
- Admission volume is significantly higher during the middle days of the month, creating resource pressure on hospitals.
- Elective admissions are flexible and can be rescheduled without affecting emergency care.
- Shifting elective patients from high-workload days to low-workload days helps balance hospital workload and improve resource utilization.
